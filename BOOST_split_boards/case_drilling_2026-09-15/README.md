# Case floor drilling, rev B (2026-09-16)

`case_floor_drilling.svg` (import into CAD) and `case_floor_drilling.html` (view in a browser).
Also at <https://claude.ai/artifact/9NmVvxbMB9MiJs8sqsNDn4>.

Eight blind tapped holes in the case floor, in box coordinates (box = board + 22, -28; datum is the inside
top-left corner of the 128 x 90 x 33 mm case):

- four M2.5 x 0.45 for the FET tab screws at the M1, M8, M9 and M10 tab holes, 6.0 mm of full thread minimum
  (M2.5 x 12 pan head, 4.9 mm engaged);
- four M3 x 0.5 for the H5-H8 standoffs that carry the board and the control card, 7.0 mm of full thread
  minimum (6 mm nylon stud, which must not bottom).

The floor is 10 mm thick and the case is sealed: blind holes, drill depth 8.5 mm maximum, bottoming tap, light
90 degree countersink to thread diameter. Nylon threads in aluminium: hand-tight only.

Coordinates come from the placed board, via `case_floor_holes.json`, regenerated by `build_drill.py`.
Source placement: `replace_2026-09-14/tools/placement/BOOST_power_p15_WIP_NOT_FOR_FAB.kicad_pcb` (p15_b10_k2).
The drawing is not to scale: work from the hole table.
