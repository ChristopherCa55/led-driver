## 3. Things you should know

1. **KiCad 10 does not apply the shipped Default netclass.** `BOOST_control.kicad_pro` says Default 0.15 / 0.15, but
   KiCad 10 loads 0.20 / 0.20, its built-in default.
   - The cause: the file's netclass entries have no `priority` field, which KiCad 10 expects. I added the field in a
     scratch copy and KiCad then honours 0.15.
   - So far the card has effectively been checked at 0.20 mm. I route to 0.20, the stricter value.
   - Restoring 0.15 would loosen what KiCad enforces today, so it is your call (§5 C). The power board's project file
     probably has the same issue: its router notes "DRC applies 0.2 mm to pads".
2. **J9 is a wall on all eight layers.** Its 2 mm pads leave 0.54 mm gaps along a row. Removing the unused inner rings
   buys nothing: KiCad then counts a foreign track crossing the ring area as a short. The rings stay.
3. **Placement:** D24 moved 0.1 mm north, the only move. Its silk outline touched D1 and D27. D27 stays put, so D27 →
   U104.1 is still 2.50 mm.
4. **DRC rule added (tightening):** 0.3 mm from copper to non-plated holes. DRC treats J9's tie slots as board edge,
   and the fills stopped at the 0.2 mm hole clearance.
5. **Silk:** five references have no legal spot within 1.5 mm of their parts: R105, R25, R35, R43, R94. The silk pass
   moves them to the Fab layer (assembly drawing) instead of onto copper or far from the part. Two others moved on
   silk. After that pass: 0 silk warnings.
6. **Via-in-pad** is used (98 on the best board), as ROUTING_SPEC 2 allows (filled, capped vias are free on 6+ layers).
   All vias are 0.5 / 0.3; the drill ≥ 0.3 DRU rule holds.
7. **Layer plan:** 0 violations. No logic on In5, no analog on In2, nothing on the GND planes, and only
   5V / analog_5V / 12V on In3.

## 4. What blocks: getting the pads out on a double-sided card

The best board (`work/sig_15p.kicad_pcb`, after six repair rounds) has 24 unconnected pairs in 17 nets. The map
`renders/unrouted_best.png` shows round 2, with 29.
- Most are analog, around U106 / U22–U24 / U26 and the J11 sense lines Vout_1/2 and Output1/2_drain.
- A few are logic (Net-(U102C-A), U26 RESET).
- It also has 4 edge-clearance errors, from tracks routed before the edge fix (§3); a fresh run won't have them.

In2 and In5 are **not** full: the plots show free space on both. What fails is getting a pad to an inner layer. With
parts on both sides (68 top, 92 underneath), a through via needs a free spot on F.Cu *and* B.Cu at once, and around the
SOICs there often isn't one.
